The human interface to Tomato

Message the machine.

Envelop is how you text a computer. It takes the form of a messenger, but the point is the path to a homemade CPU in a dorm, not another chat app with a bot contact.

Text Tomato in EnvelopSee what happens after Send

01 / After you press Send

Your browser does not pretend to be the computer.

Envelop keeps the message queued until a nearby verified bridge can reach the Envelop app inside Tomato OS. The Tomato CPU receives it there. A reply comes back with its origin attached.

When that lease is live, Tomato is awake in Philadelphia. When it isn’t, Tomato is sleeping — leave a message, or talk to Virtual Tomato. Envelop never converts an uncertain hardware attempt into a simulated success.
