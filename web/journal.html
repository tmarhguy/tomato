First phase of assembly.

The DigiKey box landed. Every 74ACT151 is down on one board, and so are the adders. Seven LEDs. Copper next to the sim.

Dispatch · Lot 07 · open the full entry

After weeks of schematics and KiCad renders, the silicon is on the desk. One board has every mux placed, each 74ACT283 soldered, and the first lamps of the debug wall sitting on their pads.

The vault still holds every earlier choice — routing that broke, muxes that left, the fall back to 32-bit. Twenty-five dispatches. This is the one at the top.
