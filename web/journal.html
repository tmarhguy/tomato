Tomato works beautifully!

Rendering an HDMI signal and a GUI on a TV, running over 2,300 lines of assembly code as TomatoOS.

Dispatch · OS · open the full entry

For all these months of working, I will say today was the most thrilling, mainly because I got Tomato to render an HDMI signal and a GUI on my TV (or any screen for that matter).

The vault still holds every earlier choice — the PMOD pivot, first lights, invisible bring-up logic, the gallery cut. Thirty-three dispatches. This is the one at the top.
