Tomato

Playground · 07_alu slice

Pick an operation. Flip some bits. Read the sum.

Every Tomato ALU step is the same shape: two 74ACT151 planes program what f and g mean, then a 74ACT283 adds them with carry. out = f(A,B,C) + g(A,B,C) + cin — 524,288 opcode pairs on the word, not a fixed function table.
