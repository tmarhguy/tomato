01 ALU Set aside

270 mm transistor-hybrid predecessor. 3,488 MOSFETs. Complete on paper—not the copper that went to fab.

 02 Shift In design

32-bit barrel shifter and multiply loop. ~forty 74ACT157 muxes. Priority encoder skips zero chunks.

 03 Memory In design

Dual-port RAM, byte-lane decode, 60×80 tile framebuffer. The bus to the outside world.

 04 Register In design

256 GPR · 3-read-1-write. Instruction register sheet. Eight banks on a 32-bit word.

 05 PC In design

Program counter, stack pointer, interrupt vectors. Keyboard tables and printer I/O hooks.

 06 Bus In design

Tri-state write-back highway. 74ACT541 + 74ACT138 → 9 ns mux. Unblocks every other slice.

 07 ALU Arrived

Dual-LUT 8-bit slice. 99.95×99.80 mm. 25 logic ICs per 8b. Zero unrouted nets. Copper has arrived.

 08 Display In design

The rest of the lamp wall and VGA timing. 07 already carries its own LED bring-up panel.
