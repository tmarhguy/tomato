About me

I’m Tyrone Marhguy.

I’m a Computer Engineering student at the University of Pennsylvania, Class of 2028. I started with an 8-bit ALU and kept building toward a computer whose hardware and software I could understand myself.

Ghana → Philadelphia

tmarhguy.com The ALU GitHub

I start from first principles, find the right primitive, and iterate until the design is correct and efficient.

I’m Tyrone Iras Marhguy. Tomato is how I put that approach into practice: rethink the ALU, build a computer around it, and write the software it runs. Explore my work or read my story.

Penn Engineering, because I am a Quaker, and ENIAC sat in the Moore School a few buildings over. The Achimota mark, because that is the public school the board is allowed to remember. The Ghana flag, because the paper is printed in Ashtown Valley even when the copper lands in Philadelphia.

The work

I build computer systems from discrete MOSFETs through full ASIC tapeout. Computer Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania — architecture, RTL, and verification. I work where hardware meets software: boards, SystemVerilog, and the constrained-random environments that prove them.

Currently: Hardware Research Engineer (Fall 2026) at Fluid Silicon (RTL & emulation, Pennovation) and Software Engineer at Aragorn AI. Previously: Hardware & Firmware Engineer at Vero Electric (PCB design & board bring-up).
