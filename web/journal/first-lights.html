Dispatch · Lot 07

First lights and flux

Every mux and adder is down. The LEDs and headers too. The board lights up — enough to know nothing is shorted.

21 August 2026 · from the log

The starting point

Bad joints and an unsoldered pin

The move

Inspect and repair the assembly

The result or direction

First powered indicators

Soldering tiny components is delicate, error-prone, and slow. But the multiplexers and adders are finally down on the board — and so are the LEDs and pin headers.

The best part: the board lights up. That is enough validation to confirm nothing is shorted. Along the way there were bad joints and an unsoldered pin. Armed with a flashlight, I kicked back and examined the copper as closely as possible until the faults showed.

It works. I have not given myself a moment to celebrate — from raw ambition, to ordering the PCBs, to a soldered board on the desk. Recruiting season has already started, and the semester (hardest, and most fun so far) is just beginning.

Caps later

I left out the capacitors. They are not essential for the board to work immediately — tiny 0603s that matter for power-hungry switching, which is not the problem yet for basic LED testing. Brownouts can wait until full-speed runs without those caps in place.

Earlier: the first assembly pass. Next problem: something has to drive the datapath without becoming a throwaway FSM.
